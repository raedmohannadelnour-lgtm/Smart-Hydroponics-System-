Master switch

Shelves set to Not in use are skipped by the automatic schedule, so they stay off and save power. Manual mode can still switch them on for demonstrations; they turn off again when Manual mode ends. The setting is kept on the device through restarts.

Pump (maintenance)

For emptying the tank or filling the shelves. Pump ON keeps the pump running until you switch it off, even if the tank reads empty. Pump OFF keeps it off. With neither selected, the pump follows the schedule.
